School Meeting Checklist

Turn a general hope into a clear agreement.

Initial draft · 25 November 2026

Download PDF

Before the meeting

Invite the student’s input in an accessible form. Gather strengths, interests, observed barriers and supports that have helped. Share only information needed for planning.

Questions to bring

What music opportunities already exist? What makes them difficult to access? Which adjustments can we try? Who provides supervision? What are the sound, fee, instrument and transport arrangements?

Protect the purpose

Is music a subject, a support or another activity here? Can agreed support remain available when other work is unfinished? What is the backup if the room or adult is unavailable?

Write down the agreement

Opportunity: ____________________
Coordinator: ____________________
Time and place: ____________________
Choices and adjustments: ____________________
Pause or stop signal: ____________________
Backup plan: ____________________

Set the review

Review date: ____________________
Student’s preferred feedback method: ____________________
What would make us continue, change or stop? Include well-being and learning, not just compliance.
