“What Do I Bring to the Meeting?”

One example, one useful request and somewhere to write the agreement. No research dissertation required.

Download the one-page handout

Before: bring your child into the conversation.

Ask what they want adults to understand. They can write, draw, record a thought or ask you to speak for them. Bring a strength, a barrier and something that has helped.

During: ask for something concrete.

“Could spare worksheets be available without asking?” “What would help them join band?” “Who could help us explore a school music program?” Choose the question that fits your situation.

Before you leave: check the details.

What will the school provide? Who will show your child how to use it? When and where? What is the backup? Choose a review date and a way for your child to tell the team how it is going.
