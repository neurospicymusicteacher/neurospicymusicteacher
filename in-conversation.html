With Lesley Moffat · Season 6, Episode 10 · 24 min 51 sec

Lesley and I talk about practical ways to support students with different learning needs in the band room, while building systems that make teaching more sustainable.

  • Making accessibility part of everyday classroom design.
  • Understanding behaviour as communication.
  • Supporting student independence without adding unnecessary work.

About Band Director Boot Camp

Hosted by Lesley Moffat, Band Director Boot Camp brings music educators together for practical conversations about teaching, productivity, and wellbeing. The show explores ways to build strong music programs while making a teaching career more sustainable.

Through the mPowered Educator, Lesley also shares professional learning, coaching, and tools to support busy music educators.
