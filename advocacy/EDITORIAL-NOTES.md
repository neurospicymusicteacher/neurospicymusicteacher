## Content and maintenance

- Ten article drafts are in `articles/`. `content.json` records the initial article copy and summaries; edit both the HTML and data when revising.
- Every article includes the agreed perspective note. Do not remove the distinction between clinical advice, educational interpretation and lived experience.
- `research/sources.json` records citation metadata, evidence type, limitations and the scope of the source check. Abstract checks are explicitly identified and are not full-text appraisals.
- Failure First is Sarah's educational approach, informed by related research; it has not been independently validated by the cited studies.
- Singing may offer reprieve from worry; it is not established as making worry impossible. Music education, listening and clinical music therapy must remain distinct.
- The original uploaded transcript.txt was unavailable in the accessible conversation attachments and workspace. Sarah supplied the red-shirt analogy directly during this session; it is included in Failure First. No transcript quotations or unverified personal classroom anecdotes were invented. Reconcile voice and examples with the transcript when available.
- The Loberg and Koelsch focused-listening preprint was located by DOI, but the original manuscript could not be inspected. It is flagged for follow-up and not used to support claims.
- Printable content is in `handouts/`; individual PDFs, the complete packet, an editable generic letter and a ZIP are in `downloads/`. Update the HTML, data and PDFs together when changing handout text. The generic letter was preserved from the earlier toolkit.
- No student information or identifying recipient details are included.
