“Where Do We Even Start?”

A quick start for parents asking for PDA support, help across classes, music inclusion or a new school music program.

Download the one-page handout

1. Pick the thing that needs to change.

Does staff need to understand your child’s PDA profile? Is getting help too public? Is music missing or hard to access? Choose one starting point. You do not need to solve the whole school experience before sending an email.

2. Bring one real example.

“When they forget a worksheet, they have to ask in front of everyone. They get stuck and the lesson moves on.” That tells the team more than “They need to be more independent.”

3. Ask for something the adults can provide.

“Could there be spare copies in a known place, with someone showing them how to use it?” For music, that might be an instrument loan, an adapted part or teaching time for a new class.

4. Write down who will do what.

Agree on a first step, the adult responsible, a backup and a review date. Ask your child what changed for them. Quietly getting through the day is only part of the picture.

Words you can borrow

Could we meet to discuss [barrier] and try [support], so my child can [what they want to do]?
