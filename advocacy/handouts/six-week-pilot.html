“What Could We Try Over Six Weeks?”

A flexible starting plan for a school support or music opportunity. Pick a review date, not an independence deadline.

Download the one-page handout

Before you start: make it usable.

Agree on what you are trying, why the student wants it, who provides help and what happens if the first plan is unavailable. Choose a realistic time. There is no magic number of minutes.

Weeks 1-2: listen, show and try.

Ask about interests and barriers. Show the student where resources live and how to get help. Offer a small, workable first experience. Change anything that adds unnecessary pressure.

Weeks 3-4: keep what helps.

Build on a chosen activity or useful routine. Keep adult support available. In music, make room for actual teaching and progress on something the student wants to learn.

Weeks 5-6: ask what changed.

Can they take part and get what they need? What do they enjoy? What leaves them exhausted? Ask the student and family, then agree what continues, changes or stops. Six weeks passing does not mean support expires.

Words you can borrow

We will try: ____________________
Lead adult: ____________________
Review together on: ____________________
