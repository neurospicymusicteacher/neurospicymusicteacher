Program Overview

Practical support for the real rhythm of the school year.

The First Downbeat is practical, realistic, and built around the actual rhythm of the school year. Through live classes, recorded sessions, resources, checklists, guided practice, discussion, and optional one-on-one support, participants will build a beginner band program that fits their students, timetable, school context, teaching style, and capacity.

The course is grounded in one important reminder: there is no one right way to teach beginner band, and you do not have to know everything before you begin.

How does registration work?

Review registration, payment, invoices, receipts, and First Chair availability.

Click for registration info ✨

To register, complete the online registration/application form. First Chair Mentorship is limited to 4 teachers, so that tier may involve a short application or confirmation step before your space is held.

After your registration is received, an invoice will be sent for the course fees. Payment is accepted by e-transfer. Credit card payments are not currently accepted.

Receipts will be provided for all registrations. Participants may use receipts for school professional development funding, reimbursement requests, or tax records where applicable. Please check with your school, district, accountant, or tax professional to confirm eligibility.
